## Status / verdict

- `continue` — worth a full 8-page outline / prototype test.
- `narrow` — keep, but reduce the claim or scene.
- `pivot` — change the frame or mechanism.
- `kill` — stop; record why.
- `exhausted` — all 5–8 directions fell back to existing families.

## Rules

- One changed variable per run.
- New selected position must differ from prior selected positions on at least 2 of: Who / gap / mechanism / promise.
- Same family: max 2 selected positions; a third is `variant` or `killed`.
- If 8/10 selected positions share one family, stop and report convergence.
- The ledger is the source of truth; do not count from memory.
