## 中文简短介绍

给设计课题生成不同方向、定位卡和质疑记录，并交付可点开、筛选、离线分享的想法地图。

## English short description

Explore a design brief through distinct directions, positioning cards and recorded challenges, then browse and share an interactive offline HTML map.

## 中文详情

课题有了，方向还没想清楚？把题目交给点子地图。

Idea Atlas 面向设计学生、教师和设计团队，用于设计前期的发散、比较和筛选。它会探索课题的不同读法，展开设计方向，把每个想法写成可点开的定位卡：谁会用、现在怎么做、为什么愿意换，以及方案要付出什么代价。

你可以沿着地图看分支，切到清单比较方向，筛选入围方案。点开卡片，可以查看已有依据、待验证的假设、下一步测试和放弃条件；完成过质疑的方向，还能追溯问题、考虑过的依据和最后的判断。

交付物包括可离线打开、单文件分享的 HTML 地图，以及便于继续修改的 Markdown 卡片和 JSON 数据。看中某个方向后，直接报出卡片编号，就能继续发展它。

只有一个题目也能开始。有调研笔记、草图或限制条件时，可以一起提供。仅凭题目生成的内容用于提出假设和安排验证，入围结果供你选择。

内容默认沿用你提问时的语言，也可以主动指定。地图界面默认英文，支持 English / 中文 切换；切换只改变按钮、筛选项和页签，不翻译或改写原始内容。

## English details

Have a design brief, but no clear direction yet? Give it to Idea Atlas.

Built for design students, educators and teams, Idea Atlas helps you explore, compare and screen ideas early in a project. It considers different readings of a brief and develops directions into positioning cards: who would use the idea, what they do today, why they might switch, and what the change would cost them.

Browse the branches, compare ideas in a list, or filter to the shortlist. Open a card to see evidence, assumptions, a proposed test and a result that would rule it out. Reviewed directions also preserve their challenges, the material considered and the resulting decision.

You receive an interactive HTML map that opens offline and can be shared as one file, plus editable Markdown cards and JSON data. Ask to continue a card by ID when you want to develop it further.

A topic alone is enough to start. Add research notes, sketches and constraints when available. Ideas generated from a brief are hypotheses to investigate; shortlist recommendations remain yours to judge.

Content follows the language of your request unless you specify another. The interface starts in English and can switch to Chinese. This changes interface labels only, preserving the original brief, ideas and review records.

## 试试这样用 / Try it like this

### 1. 开放命题 / An open brief

用点子地图探索「给牛穿的一双跑鞋」。先比较题目的不同读法，再展开设计方向，记录质疑和筛选理由，生成可点开的想法地图。

Use Idea Atlas to explore “running shoes for a cow.” Compare different readings of the brief, develop design directions, record challenges and screening decisions, and create an interactive idea map.

### 2. 产品场景 / A product situation

用点子地图探索「小厨房家庭的早餐准备能否移到餐边柜」。比较用户场景、现有替代和清洁代价，推荐值得先验证的方向。

Use Idea Atlas to explore whether breakfast preparation could move from a small kitchen to a dining-room sideboard. Compare user situations, existing alternatives and cleaning tradeoffs, then recommend directions to test first.

### 3. 整理已有材料 / Existing project material

用点子地图整理我上传的工作坊笔记和草图。归并相近想法，比较不同方向，说明先验证什么、哪些结果会让我们放弃，再生成想法地图。

Use Idea Atlas to organize my uploaded workshop notes and sketches. Group similar ideas, compare directions, explain what to test first and what results would rule them out, and create an idea map.

第三个示例需要用户提供笔记和草图；前两个可仅凭题目开始。

## 案例展示文案 / Demo captions

### 给牛穿的一双跑鞋 / Running shoes for a cow

5 种读法、18 个方向、7 张入围卡。从「谁穿这双鞋」开始，看看同一个题目如何展开不同的设计机会。点开 C01，追溯牛蹄印跑鞋经历的质疑，以及需要分别验证的问题。

Five readings, 18 ideas and seven shortlisted cards. Start with “Who wears the shoes?” and see how one brief opens up different design opportunities. Open C01 to follow the challenges to the hoof-print running shoe and the separate tests they prompted.

- 中文原稿：https://idea-atlas-demo.sturdypalm7.chatgpt.site/cow-shoes/zh.html
- English edition: https://idea-atlas-demo.sturdypalm7.chatgpt.site/cow-shoes/en.html

### 餐边柜里的第二料理中心 / A second prep station in the dining room

4 种读法、18 个方向、6 张入围卡。打开 A01「早餐发车台」的「质疑」，看看面包屑、蒸汽和「谁来擦台面」如何让方案收窄到一个带可拆洗托盘的 40 cm 早餐模块。

Four readings, 18 ideas and six shortlisted cards. Open A01, “Breakfast Station,” and select Challenges. Crumbs, steam and the question of who wipes the counter narrow the idea to a 40 cm breakfast module with a washable tray.

- 中文原稿：https://idea-atlas-demo.sturdypalm7.chatgpt.site/sideboard/zh.html

### 案例来源说明 / Demo provenance

两组案例均由 Hermes 搭配 DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 生成，分别使用 Idea Atlas 0.14.1（牛跑鞋）和 0.13.0（餐边柜）。展示页面统一使用 1.0.0 前端，英文版为中文原稿的独立译本。卡片内的测试均为待执行计划。以上是技能方法与交互形式的示例，不是 WorkBuddy 生成效果或兼容性测试结果。

Both examples were generated in Chinese with Hermes and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017, using Idea Atlas 0.14.1 for the cow-shoe brief and 0.13.0 for the sideboard brief. Both use the 1.0.0 viewer; the English editions are separate translations of the originals. Tests described in the cards are proposals. These demos illustrate the workflow and interface, not WorkBuddy-generated results or compatibility tests.
