Idea Atlas v1.0.0

Turn a design brief into an offline map of competing ideas. Each card records the audience, its current alternative, the proposed change, the evidence and a test that would make you drop the idea. Reviewed cards also show how a challenge changed the decision.

[Try the demo](https://idea-atlas-demo.sturdypalm7.chatgpt.site/) · [中文案例入口](https://idea-atlas-demo.sturdypalm7.chatgpt.site/zh.html)

- Download `idea-atlas-skill-1.0.0.zip` to install the single skill folder in Codex or Hermes.
- Download `idea-atlas-1.0.0.zip` for both illustrated demos in Chinese and English, editable manifests, the optional positioning companion and instructions.
- The English / 中文 control changes the interface. Each atlas retains its source wording.
